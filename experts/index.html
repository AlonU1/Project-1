Bio

Dr Alon Urlainis is a Senior Lecturer in the Department of Civil Engineering at Ariel University. He completed his PhD in Structural Engineering at Ben-Gurion University of the Negev in 2019, with a thesis on the probabilistic risk appraisal and mitigation of critical infrastructures for extreme events, supervised by Prof. Igal M. Shohet. He also holds a BSc in Structural Engineering from Ben-Gurion University.

His research focuses on digital construction, infrastructure resilience, and artificial intelligence in construction. He develops BIM-based workflows, probabilistic risk assessment methods, and AI-driven tools to support engineering decision-making throughout the project lifecycle. This work spans the resilience of critical infrastructure under earthquakes and other extreme events, automated construction-site monitoring, progress assessment, defect and safety detection, digital building logbooks, and machine-learning applications in civil engineering.

Dr Urlainis received the Best Paper Award at the Creative Construction Conference 2025 in Zadar, Croatia, and three consecutive Excellence in Teaching Awards from Ariel University (2021–2023). Before joining Ariel University he taught at Sami Shamoon College of Engineering and at Ben-Gurion University of the Negev.
