About

Bridging advanced computational methods with practical engineering challenges

Dr. Alon Urlainis is a Senior Lecturer in the Department of Civil Engineering at Ariel University. His research focuses on digital construction, infrastructure resilience, and artificial intelligence in construction. He develops BIM-based workflows, probabilistic risk assessment methods, and AI-driven tools to support engineering decision-making throughout the project lifecycle.

His work addresses the resilience of critical infrastructure under earthquakes and other extreme events, automated construction-site monitoring, progress assessment, defect and safety detection, digital building logbooks, and machine-learning applications in civil engineering.

Dr. Urlainis holds a Ph.D. in Civil Engineering from Ben-Gurion University of the Negev. His research and teaching bridge advanced computational methods with practical engineering challenges, aiming to promote safer, more resilient, and more efficient construction and infrastructure systems.

Research

Research Interests

Four research domains, coded like disciplines in a drawing set

B

BIM

Building Information Modeling as a working environment for risk and design — BIM-based seismic risk assessment at component level, building permit coordination for urban metro projects, and digital building logbooks.

BIM-based risk assessment Permit coordination Digital building logbooks Early-design uncertainty
A

AI & Machine Learning

Machine learning coupled with engineering analysis — transfer learning for tunnel support design, ML with numerical geotechnical models, and large language models applied to construction data.

Transfer learning ML + numerical analysis Large language models AI in construction
R

Risk Assessment

Probabilistic risk appraisal of critical infrastructures under extreme events — seismic fragility curves for oil stations, power grids, and transportation networks, loss and damage assessment, and multi-hazard mitigation.

Seismic fragility curves Infrastructure resilience Loss & damage assessment Corrosion-adjusted fragility RMIR indicator
C

Construction Management

Management and safety of the construction process — safety climate in small and medium enterprises, optimal allocation of safety resources, and service-life prediction with life-cycle cost analysis of building components.

Safety climate in SMEs Safety resource allocation Service life prediction Life-cycle costs
