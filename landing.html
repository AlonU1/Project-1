About

Dr. Alon Urlainis is a Lecturer in the Department of Civil Engineering at Ariel University. He earned his Ph.D. in Structural Engineering from Ben-Gurion University of the Negev in 2019, with a dissertation on the probabilistic risk appraisal and mitigation of critical infrastructure for extreme events.

His interdisciplinary research bridges structural engineering, construction management, and emerging technologies — developing robust risk mitigation frameworks for the facilities societies depend on most.
